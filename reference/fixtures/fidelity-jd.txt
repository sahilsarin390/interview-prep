Analyst, Data Analytics & Insights — Analytics, Research & Data (ARD)

About the team
The Analytics, Research & Data (ARD) organization is built to anticipate client
needs and shape future client behavior through data, analytics, and AI. We partner
across the business to enhance client experiences and to build AI-driven solutions
that turn data into decisions.

What you'll do
- Translate ambiguous business questions into testable hypotheses and validate them
  through analysis and experimentation.
- Surface insights, opportunities, and risks, and address the "so what" and the
  "what if" — not just report numbers.
- Build compelling, executive-ready visualizations and tell impactful data stories
  that drive decisions.
- Translate findings into actionable recommendations and recommend decisive actions,
  even under ambiguity and incomplete data.
- Apply critical thinking and structured problem-solving to analytical investigations.
- Partner cross-functionally with business, product, and technology stakeholders.

What we're looking for
- 3.5+ years of experience delivering measurable business impact through analytics.
- A degree in Economics, Statistics, Finance, or a related quantitative field; an
  MBA or MS is preferred.
- Strong SQL; hands-on experience with Snowflake and Tableau.
- Experience turning complex data into clear narratives for senior stakeholders.
- Exposure to experimentation / hypothesis testing is a plus.
- Interest in financial services and in applying AI responsibly.

Our values
We are Brave, Bold, and Curious, anchored on Integrity and Trust. We put the
customer first in everything we do.
